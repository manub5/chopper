## Intent and approach

MIT was rejected because it permits commercial software derivatives. A general
noncommercial license was also rejected: commercial audio production, sample
packs and finished music must remain permitted, including for companies.

Approved approach: a custom source-available software license restricting
commercialization of Chopper and software derivatives, not commercial use of
audio outputs. This is not an OSI-approved open-source license. The wording
below is user-approved, not legal advice or a guarantee of enforceability; legal
review is recommended before relying on it.

The root LICENSE is authoritative; its body matches the approved text below.
Third-party dependencies retain their own licenses.

---
